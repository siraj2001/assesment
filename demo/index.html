Architecture & Scale-to-Billions Design

The SkyPoints platform processes billions of airline loyalty records per day using a robust Medallion Architecture across Snowflake, Object Storage (S3/ADLS), and distributed processing:

1. Bronze (Raw Landing): Ingests high-throughput unparsed pipes and JSON into VARIANT columns with zero transformation to prevent ingestion backpressure.
2. Silver (Cleansed Staging): Resolves unpadded date strings (e.g. 3051985 -> 03051985), calculates derived Age and Stale_Member flags, and diverts corrupted records to a Dead-Letter Queue (DLQ).
3. Gold (Country Marts & Facts): Windowed deduplication (ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY member_id ORDER BY last_flight_date DESC)) guarantees "Latest Record Wins" when members migrate countries.
4. Redemptions Fact & Safe Join: 1-to-many transaction flattening combined with 1-to-1 member aggregations completely avoids Cartesian fan-out bugs.
